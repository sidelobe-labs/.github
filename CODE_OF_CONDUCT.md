# Code of conduct

Sidelobe aims to keep project spaces professional, constructive and welcoming.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss ideas and implementation choices rather than attacking people.
- Assume good faith while still being clear about technical or product concerns.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption and abusive behaviour are not acceptable.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

For a private conduct concern, contact [hello@sidelobe.dev](mailto:hello@sidelobe.dev).
